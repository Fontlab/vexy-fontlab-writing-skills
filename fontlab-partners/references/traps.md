# Partner pages: worked checks

## Follow a resource into its use

Fictional press-pack facts: the pack contains a product logo in SVG, one PNG
screenshot and a caption in TXT. The caption names the screenshot's version.
No licence, dimensions or download URL is supplied. This is a writing exercise,
not a description of an existing FontLab pack.

Developed draft:

> Start with the screenshot and keep its caption beside it. The caption names
> the version shown, so that detail can travel with the image into your story.
> The pack also contains an SVG product logo; the screenshot is a PNG and the
> caption is a TXT file.
>
> [CONFIRM LICENCE: permitted use and required attribution]
> [CONFIRM DOWNLOAD: verified pack URL]

The passage follows one image from inspection to its proposed use. It explains
why the caption belongs nearby without inventing a licence or publication
permission. The unresolved conditions remain visible before this becomes
published copy.

Compact inventory:

> SVG product logo, PNG screenshot and TXT caption naming the screenshot's
> version. [CONFIRM LICENCE] [CONFIRM DOWNLOAD]

The inventory suits a small space beside a download. The developed version
suits an introduction. Compare their facts before choosing their pace; neither
gets to claim print resolution, editable artwork or unrestricted use.

## Give a question a direct answer

Invented program terms: commission applies to a completed first purchase;
renewals are excluded. Rate and attribution window are unknown.

Question: “Do renewals qualify?”

> No. Commission applies to a completed first purchase; renewals are excluded.

That answer is complete for the supplied question. A full commission overview
would also need the missing rate and attribution terms. Adding those fields
to this narrow answer would obscure the decision the reader came to make.

## Check the path as carefully as the sentence

Verify the following in the target checkout and applicable evidence:

- Page, FAQ, signup and asset inputs produce the outputs being inspected.
  Regenerate partials and `docs/`; compare README paths with build code.
- Rates, attribution periods, eligibility, payout rules, prices, approval times
  and contacts have current support. Repeated page copy supplies no extra proof.
- Every applicable offer copy agrees, including FAQ answers and all three CTA
  bands. Preserve each band's own relative links and markup.
- A FAQ's `question` exists in generated and rendered output. The loader can
  skip a missing question while the build succeeds. New categories require
  the generator's ordering and label constants.
- Signup Markdown has a matching image basename. Inspect any skipped slide,
  the rendered sequence and the title used for image alt text.
- `src_assets/assets.txt` lists pack names; each pack has a `path` column in
  `assets.csv`. Strict mode checks listed inputs, not every page URL.
- Changed links reach the intended ZIP, whose contents match the description.
  Private working files remain outside the distributed pack.
- Product-generation identifiers change only within an authorized migration,
  with matching source paths, manifests, links and regenerated outputs.
- Product-name spacing and existing menu/footer components remain appropriate.
  Local checks and deployment results are reported separately.

## Practice the second pass

Revisit the press-pack draft after checking an actual archive. Compare every
promised file and property with what is present. Then follow the image through
the prose: does the caption explain a useful relationship, or merely repeat
the inventory? Keep that relationship in a developed introduction. In a narrow
download row, keep the exact identifiers and conditions the reader needs.
