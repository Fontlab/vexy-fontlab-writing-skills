# Balanced editing: worked cases

## Worked edit: identify and join the jobs

Evidence packet: in Vexy Lines, masks define where layer fills appear.
Transparent areas reveal a fill; opaque areas hide it. A portrait can use
separate fills for face, hair, and background. No time saving is documented.

Draft with defects:

> Unleash effortless portraits in seconds. You can give the face, hair, and
> background different styles. Regarding mask functionality, transparent
> areas reveal the fill and opaque areas hide it. This is game-changing.

Revised copy:

> Give the face, hair, and background their own drawing styles. In Vexy Lines,
> use a mask to keep each layer's fill within its own region. Transparent
> areas reveal the fill; opaque areas hide it.

The first line keeps the supported creative invitation. The second connects
that invitation to the mechanism. The third explains the same object. The
edit removes unsupported ease and speed, the awkward register announcement,
and empty praise. It preserves the technical distinction.

## Worked edit: preserve a mixed sentence

This is a fictional exercise. The supplied facts say a preview command shows
only selected objects; the command does not change the document.

Draft:

> Preview only the selected objects so you can inspect that part of the
> drawing. The preview command does not change the document.

Keep the draft unchanged. The first sentence joins a precise scope with a
supported purpose. The second states a boundary. Splitting every clause or
adding a persuasive closing line would not improve the result.

## Worked edit: do not smooth away a restriction

This is an invented offer exercise. The verified terms are €49 for version 3
owners; version 2 owners are not eligible. No deadline is supplied.

Draft with defects:

> Your upgrade is only €49, so everyone can join in. Version 2 is different.

Revised copy:

> If you own version 3, the upgrade costs €49. Version 2 owners are not
> eligible.

The original reads emotionally but its job is to state terms. Factual rules
govern the whole passage. The edit restores eligibility and does not add a
deadline, a purchase link, or a consolation pitch.
