Use newer supplied project data when it disagrees with this snapshot.

    A status of do-not-translate protects the English spelling; it does not approve a draft English name. The definition says what the term means in FontLab; the note says why the translation was chosen or how to inflect it. The fallback original term is a plain English phrase to translate instead of the term when the term itself will not travel (stem: main stroke; overshoot: optical surplus).

    Type design¶

    English Fallback Thai Status Note Definition
    ascender upward extension หางบน proposed the SSRU typography textbook (Kwanjai Sukkon) glosses Ascender as หางบน and Descender as หางล่าง; Thai type people also call the rising strokes of ป ฝ ฟ หาง. The loan แอสเซนเดอร์ is unattested in foundry prose. An ascender is the part of a lowercase letter that extends above the x-height, as in b or d. Ascender height describes the intended alignment of these parts and may differ from cap height. Do not confuse this design measurement with similarly named vertical metrics in exported font tables. Line spacing depends on those fields and on the application using them.
    baseline writing line เส้นฐาน proposed Cadson Demak articles (cadsondemak.com), Adobe th and the Thai type anatomy taught in schools use เส้นฐาน. Apple's แนวหลัก rejected. A baseline is a reference line used to align text. In conventional horizontal Latin font design, the baseline is at y = 0, with ascenders above it and descenders below it. Other scripts and layout systems can use different baseline conventions. Identify the relevant baseline when discussing alignment across scripts or vertical text.
    bowl round belly วงโค้ง proposed the SSRU typography textbook (Kwanjai Sukkon) defines Bowl as วงโค้ง. The corpus pick ชาม is the tableware sense. A bowl is a rounded stroke that encloses a counter, as in b, d or p. Its shape, thickness and junctions affect the letter's balance. The appropriate number of curve segments depends on the design. Nodes at extrema can help editing and hinting, but a fixed segment count does not guarantee compatible masters or good interpolation.
    cap height capital-letter height ความสูงตัวพิมพ์ใหญ่ proposed No fixed Thai term; fallback 'capital-letter height' translated with Apple's ตัวพิมพ์ใหญ่ for capitals. Cap height is the distance from the baseline to the top of a flat capital such as H. Rounded or pointed capitals may extend beyond it through overshoot. Record the intended value in Font Info. Cap height describes a design measurement; alignment zones and line-spacing metrics are separate settings, even when their values are related.
    character อักขระ proposed Apple, Microsoft and Cadson Demak articles (cadsondemak.com) use อักขระ for a character; kept distinct from รูปอักษร (glyph). A character is an abstract unit of written text, such as a letter or punctuation mark. A glyph is its visual representation. The relationship is not one to one: several characters can form a ligature, and a character can have several glyph forms. A user-perceived character can also consist of several Unicode codepoints. Keep characters, codepoints and glyphs distinct when discussing encoding or text processing.
    contour คอนทัวร์ proposed Cadson Demak articles (cadsondemak.com) write เส้นคอนทัวร์ (Contours) and Thai type designers keep contour as a loan. LibreOffice's ตามแนวขอบเขตวัตถุ is the text-wrap sense. A contour is an open or closed sequence of connected line and curve segments. Its nodes and control points describe its geometry. One glyph or vector object can contain several contours. A counter is an area of space, not a synonym for a contour. When explaining fill behaviour, identify the outline format and fill rule before prescribing a contour direction.
    counter enclosed white space พื้นที่ภายใน proposed Cadson Demak articles (cadsondemak.com) gloss Counter as พื้นที่ภายในตัวอักษร and the SSRU typography textbook (Kwanjai Sukkon) as พื้นที่ว่างภายใน; shortened for dense panels. The corpus pick ตัวนับ is the tally sense. A counter is the enclosed or partly enclosed space within a letterform, such as the enclosed space in an o or the open space in a c. The surrounding strokes define it. A closed counter may have an inner contour as its boundary; an open counter does not require a separate contour. Judge its size and shape together with the surrounding strokes.
    descender downward extension หางล่าง proposed the SSRU typography textbook (Kwanjai Sukkon) glosses Descender as หางล่าง; Cadson Demak articles (cadsondemak.com) speak of letters with a หาง below the baseline. A descender is the part of a letter that extends below the baseline, as in p or y. Its design height is usually recorded as a negative coordinate relative to the baseline. Exported descender metrics are separate fields used by applications for layout. Whether a deep glyph clips depends on the relevant metrics, application and text container, not on one design value alone.
    diacritical mark combining mark เครื่องหมายกำกับเสียง proposed FontForge th Unicode block names use เครื่องหมายกำกับเสียง. For Thai script the concrete words are สระ and วรรณยุกต์; Apple's long paraphrase rejected. A diacritical mark is a small sign, such as an acute or a cedilla, that attaches to a base letter to form another character. FontLab builds accented glyphs from a base and a mark through anchors, and the GDEF mark class tells the shaper which glyphs are marks. Say mark glyph for the class member and diacritical mark for the sign.
    em type body เอ็ม proposed the SSRU typography textbook (Kwanjai Sukkon) writes เอ็ม em and เอ็น en. The corpus pick is an unrelated abbreviation for minutes. The em is the square on which a typeface is designed, historically the body of the letter M in metal type. Its height equals the type size, so an em in 12 point text is 12 points. In a digital font the em is divided into UPM font units.
    extremum local extreme point จุดสุดขีด proposed Thai mathematics uses ค่าสุดขีด for an extremum; จุด makes it a point on the curve. An extremum is a point where a curve reaches a local leftmost, rightmost, topmost or bottommost position. The plural is extrema. Placing nodes at extrema can help editing and hinting. If you add nodes to masters intended for interpolation, preserve the correspondence between their contours and points. Different extremum positions alone do not make masters incompatible.
    glyph รูปอักษร proposed Apple Font Book th uses รูปอักษร throughout, and Cadson Demak articles (cadsondemak.com) write กรอบของรูปอักษร (Glyph Space). FontForge's ภาพสัญลักษณ์ means pictogram; the loan กลิฟ has no foundry attestation; อักขระ is reserved for character. A glyph is one drawn shape in a font: a letter, a figure, a mark, or a piece of a composite. A character is what a reader types and a glyph is what a renderer draws, and one character can map to several glyphs while several characters can map to one. You edit a glyph in the Glyph window, where its contours, elements, anchors, and metrics all live together. The distinction matters in any sentence about encoding, since a font maps codepoints to glyphs through the cmap table.
    handle ที่จับ proposed Inkscape th uses ที่จับ for a node handle. Kept apart from จุดควบคุม (control point), which is what the file stores. A handle is an off-curve control point used to shape a curve segment. A cubic Bézier segment has two control points; a quadratic segment has one. Moving a handle changes the curve without moving its endpoints. In tools that display handle arms, the arm connects a control point to an endpoint. Name the curve type when the number or behaviour of handles matters.
    ink trap ink relief notch ร่องดักหมึก proposed Cadson Demak articles (cadsondemak.com) gloss Ink Trap as ร่องดักหมึก. An ink trap is a notch cut into a corner where strokes meet, so ink spread or rasterization at small sizes does not clog the join. FontLab makes them as a live Smart Corner variant.
    italic angle slant angle มุมเอียง proposed Derived from ตัวเอียง, the word Cadson Demak articles (cadsondemak.com) and Apple use for italic; fallback 'slant angle'. The italic angle is the slope of the upright strokes of an italic or oblique design, measured from the vertical in degrees. FontLab stores it in Font Info, uses it to slant guides and the cursor, and writes it into the post and hhea tables. It is a property of the design, not of a transformation.
    lining figures capital-height figures ตัวเลขแบบไลน์นิง proposed Cadson Demak articles (cadsondemak.com) write ตัวเลขแบบไลน์นิ่ง; tone mark dropped per Royal Society transliteration. Apple's ตัวเลขแบบเส้น ('line numerals') is a mistranslation. Lining figures are digits that share the cap height and sit on the baseline, so they align with capitals in tables and headlines. The lnum feature selects them where a font defaults to oldstyle figures.
    node โหนด proposed Loan found in Thai vector software; Thai font makers say จุด, which is also 'point' and 'dot', and Adobe's จุดยึด is taken by anchor. Blender's จุดต่อ rejected. A node is a point on a contour where segments meet, and its type decides how they meet. A sharp node lets two segments arrive at any angle. A smooth node keeps the handles collinear so the curve passes through without a kink. A tangent node joins a line to a curve smoothly. A G2 node matches curvature as well as direction. You select a node and drag it, or nudge it, and the segments on both sides follow. Put nodes at the extremes so hinting and interpolation have something to hold.
    overshoot optical surplus ระยะทด proposed Cadson Demak articles (cadsondemak.com) use the verb ทด for optical compensation, including overshoot (S ไม่ทด overshoot); ระยะทด is the amount compensated. Close to the fallback 'optical surplus'; no noun is attested. Overshoot is the extension of a rounded or pointed shape beyond an alignment line so that it appears as tall as a flat shape. For example, an o may extend above the x-height and below the baseline. Its amount depends on the design and intended size. PostScript alignment zones can describe these overshoot regions for hinting; there is no universal percentage to apply.
    proportional figures natural-width figures ตัวเลขแบบสัดส่วน proposed Apple CoreText th (Proportional Numbers). Proportional figures have individual advance widths, so a 1 takes less room than an 8. They read well in running text; tabular figures share one width for columns. The pnum feature selects them.
    segment เซกเมนต์ proposed Royal Society loan for a segment; Apple's ส่วน is too general for a contour segment. A segment is the portion of a contour between two on-curve endpoints. It may be a straight line or a curve. A quadratic Bézier segment has one off-curve control point; a cubic segment has two. Describe the segment type and the relevant editing command rather than assuming that every curve has two handles or that changing a node type performs every conversion.
    serif เชิง proposed Cadson Demak articles (cadsondemak.com) (เชิง, เชิงฐาน), Apple Font Book (แบบมีเชิง) and the SSRU typography textbook (Kwanjai Sukkon) (เส้นเชิง) all build on เชิง. A serif is the finishing stroke at the end of a stem or arm. Its shape sorts a face into the familiar classes: oldstyle, transitional, Didone, slab. You draw a serif once and reuse it as a component or an element, which keeps a family consistent and makes a late change cheap. Serifs are small, so they take the most damage at low resolution, which is where hinting and careful alignment zones earn their place.
    sharp แหลม proposed Blender th; the plain word for pointed. คมชัด is image sharpness. Sharp describes a pointed corner or a node at which contour direction can change abruptly. Image sharpening is a different operation.
    sharp node โหนดแหลม proposed Derived from โหนด and แหลม. A sharp node joins contour segments without requiring a smooth change of direction. The handles of adjacent segments need not be collinear; a smooth node constrains their direction.
    stem main stroke เส้นลำตัว proposed the SSRU typography textbook (Kwanjai Sukkon) defines Stem as เส้นลำตัว, upright or diagonal. Cadson Demak articles (cadsondemak.com) write เส้นตั้ง (Stem), which cannot name a horizontal stem in hinting, and also the loan สเต็ม. A stem is a main stroke of a letter, usually the vertical one: the two uprights of an H, the single upright of an l. Stem width sets the weight of a face, and stems that disagree across a family break its rhythm. You measure a stem with the Measurement tool and record the standard widths in Font Info, where hinting and the stem links read them. Vextra reuses the word for the paired edges it detects in general artwork, not only in letters.
    swash flourish เส้นปลายปัด proposed Cadson Demak articles (cadsondemak.com) gloss Swash as เส้นปลายปัด. Apple's ตวัด is the shorter alternative. A swash is a decorative flourish that extends a letter, often on a capital or a terminal. Swash alternates live in the swsh feature and in glyphs with a swash suffix.
    terminal stroke ending ปลายจบ proposed Cadson Demak articles (cadsondemak.com) gloss Terminal as ปลายจบ (18 hits); the SSRU typography textbook (Kwanjai Sukkon) has ส่วนจบปลายเส้น. The corpus pick เทอร์มินัล is the command-line app. A terminal is the end of a stroke without a serif, such as the ending of a c or f. Its shape may be rounded, angled or cut off, depending on the design. Compare related terminals across a typeface at the intended reading size. The anatomical term does not prescribe how to construct or store the shape in an editor.
    UPM units per type body UPM proposed Abbreviation kept; write หน่วยต่อเอ็ม in explanatory prose. Keep numeric controls compact: LSB/RSB (French AG/AD), UPM and PPM. Expanded terms belong in explanatory help; the full Family Dimensions Units Per eM label is an intentional exception. Team UI convention in Proteus 1751b28b0. UPM, units per em, specifies how many font units make up one em. It sets the scale for outline coordinates and metrics when a renderer converts them to a text size. Values such as 1000 and 2048 are common; TrueType outlines do not require a power of two. When changing UPM, distinguish changing the scale value from rescaling the outlines and metrics.
    winding direction drawing direction ทิศทางคอนทัวร์ proposed No attestation; fallback 'drawing direction' applied to the chosen noun คอนทัวร์, as the reviewed languages do. Winding direction is the order in which a contour runs, clockwise or counter-clockwise, and it decides which areas the renderer fills. FontLab follows the PostScript convention: outer contours wind counter-clockwise and counters wind clockwise. The letter O is two contours, one each way. You check direction before you export, and reverse a contour when a counter fills solid or an outer shape turns hollow. A renderer applies the rule at draw time, so the error shows in the proof rather than in the editor.
    x-height small-letter height ความสูง x proposed Cadson Demak articles (cadsondemak.com) explain x-height as the height of the letter x and the SSRU typography textbook (Kwanjai Sukkon) as ความสูงอักษร x-height; for Thai letters the foundry says ความสูง บ. The x-height is the height of a lowercase x above the baseline, and it stands for the height of the lowercase in general. It does more for apparent size than the point size does: two faces at the same size look different when their x-heights differ. You record it in Font Info so hinting and alignment zones can use it, and you draw round letters slightly above it to allow for overshoot. Write it lowercase with a hyphen, even at the start of a sentence recast to avoid one.

    Font engineering¶

    English Fallback Thai Status Note Definition
    advance height ความสูงกรอบ proposed No attestation; fallback translated in parallel with advance width (ความกว้างกรอบ). Same concept as vertical advance, so the same term. The distance the text position advances vertically after a glyph. It is the vertical counterpart of advance width, not the height of the visible outline.
    advance width glyph box width ความกว้างกรอบ proposed No attestation; fallback 'glyph box width' translated. Cadson Demak articles (cadsondemak.com) call the glyph box กรอบของรูปอักษร; ความกว้าง alone is kept for geometric width and the width axis. Advance width is the horizontal distance allocated to a glyph before layout adjustments, measured in font units. In an OpenType font, the base values are stored in the hmtx table. Kerning and other positioning operations can adjust advances during layout without rewriting those base values. Advance width differs from the visible outline width; a space can have an advance without an outline.
    alignment zone height band for snapping โซนจัดแนว proposed No attestation; fallback translated. โซน is the Thai software word for zone, จัดแนว for align (Apple, Microsoft th). An alignment zone is a region around a common vertical alignment, such as the baseline or cap height, used in PostScript hinting. It can include the overshoot of rounded letters. A rasterizer uses zone information when fitting outlines to a pixel grid. Keep this mechanism distinct from design measurements and from the control-value and instruction mechanisms used in TrueType hinting.
    anchor attachment point จุดยึด proposed FontForge th (Anchor Lost = จุดยึดหายไป) and f0nt.com tutorials, which call anchors จุด; Inkscape's จุดสมอ rejected. Note that Adobe th uses จุดยึด for a path anchor point, which here is โหนด. An anchor is a named attachment point in a glyph that says where a mark belongs. A base letter carries a top anchor, and the accent carries a matching one, so the accent lands in the same relation on every letter that uses it. You place anchors in the Anchors and Pins panel, and FontLab uses them both to build composites and to generate mark attachment lookups in GPOS, which is what positions marks in text the font never composed itself.
    anti-aliasing edge smoothing การลดรอยหยัก proposed QCAD th; the SSRU typography textbook (Kwanjai Sukkon) has ขจัดรอยหยัก. Both name the jagged edge (รอยหยัก), the word Thai graphics users know. Anti-aliasing paints partly covered pixels in intermediate shades so a rendered outline looks smooth. FontLab's preview can show anti-aliased, monochrome or subpixel rendering so you can check how hints behave under each. Write the hyphenated form.
    autohinting automatic screen fitting การฮินต์อัตโนมัติ proposed Process noun derived from การฮินต์ (GIMP th); Thai font developers keep hinting as a loan. Autohinting generates hints for a font from its outlines and its declared stems and zones, in one pass over the glyphs. It is a starting point rather than a finish: it hints the regular cases well and leaves the awkward glyphs for you. You run it after the alignment zones and standard stem widths are correct in Font Info, because it reads them. Then you check the glyphs that matter at text sizes and correct by hand what the pass got wrong.
    axis แกน proposed Cadson Demak articles (cadsondemak.com), Google th variable font docs, Apple and Microsoft all use แกน. An axis is one dimension of font variation, such as weight, width or optical size. In an OpenType variable font, each axis has a four-character tag and a minimum, default and maximum value. Registered tags, such as wght, have defined meanings. Preserve tag spelling and case, and check the axis registry when assigning a custom tag.

G2 continuity means two segments meet with matching curvature, not merely with matching direction. G1 keeps the tangent continuous and can still show a break in the reflection where the curvature jumps. G2 removes that break. Vextra marks G2 nodes and can enforce the condition as you edit. You reach for it on long, shallow curves and on shapes that will be seen large, where the eye finds a curvature break that it would miss at text size.

You place on-curve points at extremes so hinting and interpolation have stable anchors.

    stroke เส้นขีด proposed Thai graphics software uses เส้นขีด for a stroke as opposed to a fill. Bare เส้น, the GIMP and Inkscape pick, means any line. A stroke is the line drawn along a path, with a weight, a colour, and an alignment that puts it inside, outside, or centred on the path. It is not the fill, which covers the area the path encloses. A stroke cap is the shape at the open end of a stroked path: flat, square or round. Start and end caps can differ, and a cap can be linked so both ends match.

A tonal map is the reading of brightness across a source image that a fill uses to decide where to put geometry. Dark areas widen a strip or crowd a pattern; light areas narrow or thin it. You control the mapping with the Brightness, Contrast, and Source Levels rows, which change what counts as dark before any geometry is drawn.
